# Enterprise IT Services & Digital Transformation Analytics

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI project designed to analyze enterprise business performance across multiple functions, including workforce, clients, projects, sales, finance, IT operations, and cloud and AI initiatives.

## Business Areas Covered
- **Executive Dashboard:** Revenue, profit, profit margin, and business performance.
- **Workforce Analytics:** Employee performance, utilization, and attrition.
- **Client Analytics:** Customer satisfaction, feedback, and client risk.
- **Project Analytics:** Budget vs. actual cost, project profitability, and delivery status.
- **Sales & Revenue:** Sales pipeline, opportunity value, and expected revenue.
- **Finance & Billing:** Invoices, payments, taxes, and overdue amounts.
- **IT Operations & SLA:** Incidents, resolution time, SLA compliance, and penalties.
- **Cloud, Training & AI:** Cloud savings, AI ROI, model accuracy, and training completion.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

## Key Deliverables
- Multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard
- Business KPI monitoring
- Operational and financial performance analysis
- Visual insights to support business decision-making
